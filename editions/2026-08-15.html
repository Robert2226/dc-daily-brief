01Equinix

Equinix's Munich expansion reaches topping-out, but the delivery risk now moves inside the shell

MU4.3 has completed its structural milestone and is moving into internal fit-out as part of a phased expansion of the existing MU4 campus. Topping out is visible progress, yet the remaining systems integration, controls, testing and live-campus interfaces carry much of the risk that determines when the added capacity becomes operational.

Data Center Dynamics

In Practice · Rebaseline risk when the project changes phases

At shell completion, retire structural risks that are truly closed and add fit-out risks for equipment delivery, controls integration, energization, testing access and work beside live systems. Reconfirm owners and contingency before the next gate. A milestone should change the risk picture, not merely the percent-complete number.

02Google

Google's post-incident report shows how maintenance can temporarily erase designed redundancy

A July Google Cloud disruption followed an upstream electrical fault while a redundant source was unavailable because of ongoing construction; a DRUPS failed to carry load, a chiller controller dropped offline and data-hall temperature eventually reached 44°C. The report makes the combined-risk lesson explicit: planned impairment, utility disturbance and component failures must be evaluated together rather than as independent events.

Google Cloud Service Health

In Practice · Govern maintenance by the temporary system state

Before approving work that removes redundancy, redraw the active power and cooling paths, list coincident failures the site can no longer tolerate, lower escalation thresholds and define restoration triggers. Brief the control room on the temporary failure modes. Normal operating procedures are insufficient when maintenance has changed the topology.

03Competitors

Anthropic's 20-year Kentucky lease gives TeraWulf a large contracted anchor before the campus is built

TeraWulf says the lease could generate about $19 billion over its initial term for capacity at a phased campus planned to reach 401 MW, with initial delivery targeted for the second half of 2027. The contract strengthens financing and demand visibility, but it also concentrates execution around one major tenant, a long schedule and a facility that must still be completed.

Data Center Dynamics

In Practice · Turn the anchor contract into a mutual obligation map

Separate provider milestones, customer prerequisites, acceptance tests, delay remedies, expansion options and change-control rights; show where one party's missed input blocks the other. Track concentration exposure alongside contracted revenue. A long lease reduces demand uncertainty while increasing the importance of interface governance.

04DC Infrastructure

Israel pauses large data-center grid applications after requests overwhelm realistic supply

The Electricity Authority froze processing for new facilities of 8 MW or more for 140 days after receiving a reported 19 GW of requests in two months, allowing agencies to develop allocation rules and distinguish executable projects from speculative demand. The pause demonstrates how inflated pipelines can consume planning capacity and force regulators to replace first-come processing with evidence-based prioritization.

Data Center Dynamics

In Practice · Make the interconnection request earn its place in the queue

Maintain evidence for site control, customer demand, financing, load profile, design maturity and deposit readiness; update or withdraw requests when assumptions change. Score pipeline capacity by probability instead of summing every application. Credible evidence protects both the program forecast and the utility relationship.

05AI & Compute Demand

AMD and Anthropic tie up to 2 GW of accelerator demand to a specific rack-scale platform

AMD plans to invest up to $5 billion in Anthropic while the AI company targets deployment of up to 2 GW of MI450-based Helios systems, beginning with a stated first gigawatt in the first half of 2027. The figures are forward commitments, but they show compute demand becoming a coordinated program across chips, CPUs, networking, rack integration, power and software optimization.

Data Center Dynamics

In Practice · Track the deployment as a full-stack bill of readiness

For each capacity block, align accelerator supply, rack certification, network fabric, firmware, cooling envelope, power delivery, site date and workload acceptance. Identify the component with the lowest schedule confidence. Gigawatts of demand become usable compute only when every layer arrives in a tested configuration.

06New AI Models & Releases

Gemini 3.6 Flash reaches the global Gemini Enterprise region as the prior Flash version exits

Google Cloud release notes say administrators can enable Gemini 3.6 Flash for Gemini Enterprise and Agent Designer workflows while Gemini 3.5 Flash is removed from the global region. A model transition inside an enterprise platform creates a governed migration window: teams must validate behavior, update controls and move dependent workflows before availability changes become an incident.

Google Cloud

In Practice · Treat model retirement as a dependency deadline

Inventory workflows, prompts, evaluations, guardrails and owners tied to the outgoing version; test the replacement against quality, latency, cost and tool behavior, then set an internal cutoff ahead of the provider date. Keep a rollback path where the platform permits it. Discovery is the first control in any model migration.

07Networking

Prysmian and Relativity push hollow-core fiber toward denser data-center deployment

The companies are pairing a high-fiber-count cable with hollow-core technology intended to reduce latency and improve pathway density for AI connectivity. Novel fiber may improve performance, but adoption also creates new requirements for termination, test equipment, bend limits, splicing, supplier support and field-repair competence.

Data Center Dynamics

In Practice · Qualify the installation ecosystem with the cable

Build acceptance criteria for loss, latency, density and environmental limits, then prove technicians, tools, spares, cleaning, repair times and documentation can support the design. Run a representative repair exercise before production use. A better medium is operationally ready only when the team can maintain it.

08Backend / Cloud & Data

Cloudflare formalizes human handoff inside multi-step browser automation

Browser Run can now pause an agent through structured commands, give a person a Live View to handle a blocked step and resume after completion. The feature turns human intervention from an improvised exception into an explicit workflow state, but teams still need rules for what can be handed off, what context is exposed and how completion is verified.

Cloudflare

In Practice · Design the handoff as a controlled state transition

Define allowed triggers, information shown, authorized human actions, timeout, cancellation, audit record and the condition that lets automation resume. Test abandoned and unsuccessful handoffs as well as success. Human-in-the-loop reliability depends on the protocol around the person, not simply their availability.

09Program & PM

Jira adds native approval steps that make decision governance part of the workflow record

Atlassian's July changes describe configurable approver roles, single- or multi-step chains, notifications and approval history for company-managed projects. Embedding approvals can improve accountability, but only when the organization defines which decisions need a gate, who has authority and what evidence an approval actually certifies.

Atlassian

In Practice · Define the decision before automating its approval

For each gate, state the decision, accountable approver, required evidence, response time, delegation rule and meaning of approve, reject or return. Remove gates that do not change risk or authorize action. Workflow tooling strengthens governance only when it captures real decision rights rather than ceremonial clicks.

10PgPM Growth

Map stakeholders by influence, impact and needed behavior—not title alone

At the foundation level, identify everyone who can affect or is affected by a data-center program, then record what outcome they care about, what influence they hold and what action the program needs from them. This turns a contact list into an engagement tool and exposes overlooked groups before a decision or handoff stalls.

APM · Stakeholder engagement

Design communication around the decision the audience must make

Connect the stakeholder map to a communication plan by tailoring detail, cadence, channel and explicit ask to each audience. An executive may need a recommendation and tradeoff; an Operations lead may need readiness evidence; a vendor may need a dated acceptance criterion. Effective PgPM communication moves work instead of merely reporting activity.
