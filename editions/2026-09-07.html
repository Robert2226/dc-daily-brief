02Google

September 1 release · Bigtable brings aggregate management into the console

Google's release notes mark console creation and management of aggregate column families, plus viewing and querying them in Bigtable Studio, as generally available. The new milestone concerns those interfaces, not the invention of aggregation. Bigtable aggregates can combine incoming values during writes, reducing work required when reading results. For operational reporting, the attraction is faster access to summaries; the design question is whether the chosen aggregate preserves the information you will later need.

Release notes · September 1 · Technical background · Aggregates

In Practice · A total cannot explain its own history

Two hypothetical energy feeds can produce the same daily total while having very different peaks. Choose summaries to answer a defined question, and decide separately which raw observations must remain available for investigation. A convenient console does not make that data-model decision for you.

04DC Infrastructure

Reported today · STULZ refreshes cooling for smaller edge sites

DCD's September 7 story covers TelAir 4 and WallAir 4, which STULZ says became orderable September 1. The range includes indoor and externally mounted configurations, free-cooling operation and nominal capacities from 6 to 17 kW. These are manufacturer specifications, not measured performance at a particular site. Small distributed rooms need a different selection conversation from a hyperscale liquid-cooling hall: mounting space, ambient conditions and service access can dominate the choice.

05DCOS, DCIM, BMS & Controls

September 2 agreement · Vertiv moves toward microgrid controls through UIG

Vertiv announced an agreement to acquire UtilityInnovation Group for about $1.45 billion at closing, with up to $1.15 billion in additional performance-based consideration. Its stated scope includes microgrid controls, generation orchestration and related switchgear. This is a proposed acquisition, and faster time to power is Vertiv's expected benefit rather than an independently demonstrated project outcome. The controls angle matters: coordinating generation, loads and grid interaction introduces responsibilities beyond displaying electrical measurements.

Primary announcement · September 2 · Related lesson · Control authority

In Practice · Seeing and commanding are different privileges

DCOS means Data Center Operating System; DCIM means Data Center Infrastructure Management. Their scope varies, but neither label automatically grants command authority. BMS and SCADA provide supervisory functions; electrical power monitoring measures electrical conditions; historians retain time-series records; local controllers execute control logic. Define which integration only reads status and which can change equipment behavior.

07New AI Models & Releases

September 3 release · H Company's NeoMME focuses on finding documents

H Company introduced multilingual, multimodal encoders in 260-million and 800-million parameter sizes, with Apache 2.0 checkpoints. Its retriever produces dense and late-interaction embeddings from document page images; the developer reports its own performance results. The relevance is practical: engineering knowledge lives in diagrams and tables as well as clean text. This is a retrieval-model release, not a conversational assistant launch, and the reported benchmarks do not establish accuracy on your drawings.

In Practice · Retrieve the right revision

Build a hypothetical evaluation with two nearly identical equipment diagrams from different revisions. Test whether retrieval finds the current approved page and whether the answer cites it. A model that understands page layout still needs document identity, access permissions and revision control around it.

09Backend / Cloud & Data

September 4 release · Bedrock simplifies connecting enterprise knowledge sources

AWS added user-managed setup for SharePoint, OneDrive and Confluence sources in Bedrock Managed Knowledge Base. Users can authenticate through an existing account instead of first arranging service-account credentials. That is a lower-friction setup path, not proof of equivalent permission behavior: AWS's SharePoint documentation says document-level access control is available only with Microsoft Entra ID App-Only authentication and cannot be changed after creation. Choose the access model before the prototype becomes a shared service.

AWS announcement · September 4 · Technical documentation · Authentication and ACLs · Related lesson · Permission boundaries

In Practice · Ingestion permission is only the first question

An account may be allowed to collect documents while the eventual audience has narrower rights. Test two hypothetical users with different document access and check both retrieved passages and citations. Successful synchronization does not establish correct audience authorization.
