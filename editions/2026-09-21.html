01Equinix

Deadline watch · Japan connectivity retirements arrive September 30

Equinix's public lifecycle notice, originally announced March 30, retires BI-VLAN and Bit-isle Connect in Japan on September 30, 2026. Services not migrated or canceled by that date will terminate. This is an approaching operational deadline, not a new announcement or a claim that any particular customer is behind. It is worth revisiting because migration readiness must include the application path, not just provisioning a replacement connection.

Equinix lifecycle notice

In Practice · Separate installation from migration

A replacement circuit can be live while the workload still depends on the retiring service. A useful completion statement identifies the traffic moved, the end-to-end check and the remaining dependency—not simply “new connection delivered.”

02Google

Texas battery pilot tests hourly clean-energy matching

September 21 reporting. A three-month Google-sponsored pilot with Quintrace, esVolta and LevelTen used time-stamped certificates to shift renewable-energy attributes into hours when Google's consumption exceeded its renewable supply. The partners report 9.2 GWh charged and discharged during the relevant windows. Google contracted for the environmental attributes without taking dispatch control of the batteries. This is a demonstrated commercial/accounting approach reported by the participants, not proof of round-the-clock clean power at every Google facility.

DCD pilot report

In Practice · Ask which service the battery provides

Hourly carbon matching, grid balancing and on-site ride-through solve different problems. A clean-energy contract does not establish UPS autonomy. Keep the environmental claim, dispatch rights and physical backup function separate in a program brief.

03Incidents, Reliability & Remediation

Missed coverage · Equinix identifies valve failures in September 11 NY2 fuel release

Patch's September 15 update quotes Equinix attributing the Secaucus leak to mechanical failure of two valves used during routine fuel filtration. It separately quotes NJDEP estimating 5,000 gallons released, with cleanup continuing in the affected creek. NJDEP had not determined a specific cause and reported no observed river or wildlife impacts at that update. The company explanation is therefore not a final regulator finding. A completed remediation report and demonstrated recurrence controls were not verified in this research pass.

Local report with company and NJDEP statements

In Practice · Keep the attribution attached

“The company identified two valve failures” and “the regulator determined the cause” are different statements. Preserve that distinction when briefing stakeholders; neither containment nor an early explanation proves long-term remediation is complete.

04DC Infrastructure

OData brings DeltaFlow liquid cooling to Brazil and Mexico

September 21. DCD reports phase-one installation and testing underway at SP04 in Brazil and QR03 in Mexico. The Aligned/Munters technology will work alongside existing Delta Cubed air cooling, creating a hybrid arrangement for high-density racks. The report puts the two-site project at $630 million; it does not establish completed acceptance or customer-ready capacity. The useful development is a regional deployment moving into installation and testing, not merely another cooling product announcement.

DCD deployment report

In Practice · Keep both thermal budgets

Direct-to-chip cooling can move much of a rack's heat into liquid while other components still reject heat to air. Confirm each load against its actual cooling path; a liquid-capacity rating alone does not establish that the room can support the rack.

05DCOS, DCIM, BMS & Controls

Technical reading · Connect facility telemetry to compute decisions

Equinix's July 8 architecture article describes Smart View telemetry entering NVIDIA Mission Control through its Building Management System integration. Circuit power, environmental readings and equipment state become visible beside compute information. The article also explains why coolant temperature, pressure, flow and leak detection matter for dense AI systems. This is older, explicitly dated vendor technical reading selected for today's cooling focus—not a newly released integration or independent evidence of a particular deployment's reliability.

Equinix architecture article

In Practice · Integration needs meaning, not just connectivity

A flow reading without units, sensor location or measurement type can produce a convincing but wrong dashboard. Carry those definitions through the gateway and historian. Supervisory visibility is also distinct from local control and protective functions.

06AI & Compute Demand

Amazon's generator agreement gives suppliers a longer demand signal

September 21. DCD, citing Reuters, reports approximately $2.4 billion of initial Generac deliveries over two years, with Generac expecting up to $8 billion over the longer term. The equipment is for Amazon's data-center portfolio; the report does not allocate it exclusively to AI workloads or convert it into usable compute MW. This is a substantial procurement signal, while the longer-term value remains an expectation rather than equipment already shipped.

DCD agreement report

In Practice · Do not translate dollars directly into capacity

Generator packages differ in rating, redundancy, enclosure, switchgear and service scope. Delivery value can support demand planning, but a capacity estimate needs quantities, configurations and deployment dates before it becomes credible.

07New AI Models & Releases

Kimi K3 becomes generally available on Amazon Bedrock

September 18 catch-up. AWS lists native vision, a one-million-token context window and explicit prompt caching for the open-weight model. It attributes the 2.8-trillion-parameter scale and capability claims to Moonshot AI. Bedrock availability uses cross-region inference; assess the relevant routing and service conditions before assuming a workload remains in one region. Availability and advertised context size are useful facts, not a substitute for testing output quality on your documents.

AWS release notice

In Practice · Test the task, not the headline

Use a small, public-document evaluation set with known answers and citations. Compare correctness, time and cost on the same tasks. A larger context window does not guarantee that the model reliably finds the decisive detail.

08Networking

GlobalFoundries and Marvell expand the optical supply pipeline

September 21. Their multiyear agreement expands silicon-germanium capacity in Burlington, Vermont, supporting future pluggable, near-packaged and co-packaged optical technologies. DCD reports the existing portfolio supports 200G per lane, with higher-speed generations on the roadmap. This is manufacturing and development capacity, not a claim that every roadmap product is shipping. It highlights how AI networking growth depends on specialized analog and optical supply as well as switches.

DCD optical agreement report

In Practice · Include the service boundary in the comparison

Moving optics closer to compute or switching changes packaging and potentially replacement procedures. Compare bandwidth and power alongside failure isolation, field replacement, spares and the supplier's supported maintenance model.

09Backend / Cloud & Data

ECS Express Mode adds ARM64 deployment support

September 18 catch-up. AWS now lets new and existing Express Mode services specify ARM64 for Graviton-powered compute, using the console, command-line tools, SDKs or infrastructure as code. Express Mode manages application networking, load balancing, scaling and deployment around the supplied container image. AWS advertises up to 40% better price-performance versus x86; that is a vendor claim, not an assured saving for a particular application.

AWS release notice

In Practice · Check the image's whole dependency chain

Application source may be portable while native libraries, agents or base images are not. Verify architecture support, rebuild where needed and compare a representative workload before changing a cost forecast.
