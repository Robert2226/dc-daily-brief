In Practice · Keep responsibilities distinct

DCOS means Data Center Operating System: an operational integration layer whose scope varies by implementation. DCIM means Data Center Infrastructure Management, often connecting assets, capacity and operational data. BMS and SCADA provide supervisory functions; electrical power monitoring specializes in electrical measurements; historians preserve time-series history; local controllers execute control logic. A gateway connects these domains without making their responsibilities interchangeable.
