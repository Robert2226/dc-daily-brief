01Equinix

Comcast plans last-mile ordering inside Equinix Fabric

September 10. Comcast Business says enterprises will be able to order its last-mile Ethernet through standards-based APIs within Equinix Fabric, initially at eligible locations. Comcast provisions the access connection; the stated goal is to reduce delivery from weeks to days, with optical wavelengths and other services potential extensions. This moves a difficult provider interface into the ordering workflow, but the announcement does not establish that every location is eligible or that the time-saving target has been demonstrated in production.

Comcast announcement

In Practice · Separate request latency from service lead time

An API may acknowledge an order in seconds while survey, building access or physical installation still controls completion. Compare order accepted, provider committed date and service tested as separate timestamps. That reveals whether automation removed administrative delay or merely made the remaining wait more visible.

02Google

Avid brings browser editing and searchable media workflows into its Google Cloud partnership

September 11. The companies introduce browser-based Media Composer and an upgraded Content Core using Gemini Enterprise and BigQuery, with demonstrations at IBC2026 on September 11–14. The deployment angle is the combination of remote editing, media discovery and shared data services: the user interface can move to a browser while storage throughput, network quality and access control remain essential. Treat the productivity claims as the partners’ expectations, not independently measured results.

Joint announcement

In Practice · Follow the large object

In a media workflow, ask where the original video lives, what the browser streams, where processing happens and which copy is authoritative. A lightweight client can conceal a demanding backend. Test the complete user path with representative media sizes before using a small demonstration to estimate production performance.

September 11 reporting. DCD describes financing for the Australian operator’s development pipeline, with notes maturing in September 2031. The new funding expands financial options while introducing future repayment or conversion obligations; it is not evidence that the associated facilities are already ready for customers.

DCD reporting

Theseus names Krupal Raval as CEO

September 11. The Anthropic-backed infrastructure company has appointed the former Digital Realty, Equinix and CyrusOne executive to lead its build, operate and lease strategy. Theseus was launched with Macquarie Asset Management and GIC in August; this is a leadership development in that existing platform, not another announcement of its formation.

DCD report

In Practice · Watch the operating model behind the capital

A platform serving one anchor customer can simplify demand planning while concentrating commercial exposure. Compare who funds the building, owns the compute and carries an unused-capacity risk. The same campus size can represent very different obligations for its developer and operator.

04DC Infrastructure

Nostrum’s Badajoz project advances its electrical approvals

September 11 English reporting, following DCD’s September 10 Spanish report. Preliminary administrative authorization covers part of the substation and underground-line infrastructure for the planned campus. DCD distinguishes up to 300 MW of grid connection capacity from an initial documented 170 MW total demand and 110 MW critical demand; operations are planned for 2031. These are planning and authorization figures, not energized capacity. The distinction between MW and transformer MVA also matters when reading the proposed electrical architecture.

DCD report

In Practice · Put the boundary beside the number

“30 MW” could describe utility import, an IT load or an eventual phase. Add the measurement boundary, operating condition and status to every capacity number in a comparison. Otherwise, an apparently larger option may simply include more facility overhead or more future phases.

05DCOS, DCIM, BMS & Controls

Technical briefing: Johnson Controls frames the progression from BAS control to plant optimization

Session dated September 10. Its public briefing describes a progression from Building Automation System control to supervisory Central Utility Plant optimization and then AI-enabled operation, with data quality, plant complexity and organizational readiness as prerequisites. This is a vendor educational framing, not a new product release or verified performance study; the accessible agenda does not establish the session’s detailed results. It is useful for evaluating what a proposed optimization layer actually adds to existing controls.

Public session description

In Practice · Define the objective and the constraints

An optimizer might seek lower combined chiller-and-pump power while respecting temperature, flow and equipment limits. Local controllers still execute their configured loops and protections. DCIM provides asset and capacity context; a Data Center Operating System may coordinate workflows. None of those labels alone proves that a platform can safely write a setpoint or validate its own savings.

06AI & Compute Demand

Oracle reports more than 300,000 GPUs and 850 MW delivered in its fiscal first quarter

Disclosed on the September 10 earnings call and reported by DCD September 11. Management says delivery accelerated sharply and describes customer prepayments, customer-owned hardware and supplier financing among the ways capacity is funded. This is a stronger signal of deployment activity than an undated pipeline, but the figures remain management-reported and should not be converted into a universal watts-per-GPU ratio: the hardware mix and capacity boundary are not specified by that arithmetic.

DCD earnings coverage

In Practice · Align the cash and delivery curves

A customer prepayment can reduce a provider’s immediate financing need while creating a dated service obligation. Place cash receipt, hardware payment and service commencement on one timeline. Funding certainty and delivery certainty answer different questions, even when both improve in the same quarter.

07New AI Models & Releases

DeepSeek releases V4.1-Flash and changes API routing

September 10. DeepSeek’s new model includes native visual understanding and an asymmetric architecture with different active parameter counts for input and output processing. The immediate operational detail is its migration policy: starting September 14 at 04:00 UTC, requests to the V4-Pro API identifier will route to V4.1-Flash until V4.1-Pro launches. DeepSeek’s claimed performance and cache improvements require workload-specific evaluation; keeping the same API name will not preserve the same model behavior.

IBM and NASA release a lunar foundation model and aligned scientific data

September 10. The release combines observations from multiple instruments for tasks such as crater identification and potential ice mapping. The transferable idea is a reusable model over aligned observations, rather than building a separate model for every task; its reported scientific results do not establish suitability for facility telemetry.

IBM announcement

In Practice · Record the implementation behind the alias

A test result belongs to a model version, configuration and dataset. When a provider redirects an alias, repeat the tests that matter to your workflow and retain the prior result for comparison. An unchanged endpoint can conceal a material dependency change.

08Networking

Nokia previews its next optical demonstrations for ECOC

September 10 preview; the event runs September 20–24. Nokia plans to show 1.6T linear pluggable optics, preview 448G-per-lane transmission and demonstrate data-center interconnect technologies. The distinction between demonstration and qualified deployment is central: faster lanes promise density gains, while thermal behavior, interoperability and serviceability determine whether a product fits an operating network.

Nokia preview

In Practice · Compare the replaceable unit

Moving optics nearer the switching silicon can reduce the electrical path, but service design depends on what can be replaced independently. Compare a failed optical component’s replacement scope, required outage and spare cost alongside bandwidth and power. A fast link still needs a workable repair path.

09Backend / Cloud & Data

AWS adds cross-account EBS volume clones

September 9, an uncovered boundary-day release. Amazon Elastic Block Store can now copy a shared volume into another AWS account, optionally re-encrypting it with a key in the destination account. The copy must remain in the source Availability Zone, identified consistently across accounts by its zone ID. This is useful for separate test environments, but account separation does not create geographic recovery independence or automatically remove sensitive production data.

AWS release

In Practice · Ask which failure the copy survives

A second account can separate permissions. A second volume can separate later writes. A second physical location addresses a different failure boundary. State the recovery objective before choosing the copy mechanism, then demonstrate that the application can use the recovered data.

Study storage copies and database consistency

10Program & PM

A withdrawn Italian project exposes an evidence gap in the approval process

September 11. DCD, citing Corriere della Sera, reports withdrawal of a proposed 120 MW data-center application in Travagliato. The mayor says the municipality lacked information needed to assess the environmental impact and grid connection; the developer had been approached for comment. This is attributed reporting about a contested process, not a complete root-cause investigation. For delivery leaders, it illustrates why a promised benefit and a reviewable evidence package are separate program outputs.

DCD reporting and attribution

In Practice · Distinguish disagreement from missing evidence

“We disagree with your conclusion” calls for comparing assumptions and criteria. “We cannot assess it” calls for a usable submission. Ask the reviewer to identify the unanswered question before commissioning another presentation. More advocacy rarely resolves a missing technical input.
