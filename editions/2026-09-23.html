01Equinix

Catch-up analysis · Read the assumptions behind the 190% networking ROI

September 17. Equinix published findings from a Forrester study it commissioned, combining interviews with four decision-makers into a composite organization. The modeled three-year benefits were approximately $3.1 million against $1.1 million in costs. The useful signal is where savings came from: administration, connectivity, capacity flexibility and egress. This is a sponsored composite business case—not a guaranteed return for every deployment or a new quarterly financial result.

Equinix study explanation

In Practice · Translate the baseline before importing the benefit

If your existing service already avoids a cost, you cannot claim that saving again. Build the comparison from the actual starting architecture, migration expense and operating workload; use the study to identify questions rather than copy its headline return.

02Google

Samsung C&T joins the delivery team for Google's planned nuclear supply

September 22. Kairos Power selected Samsung C&T for its 50 MW Hermes 2 demonstration project in Tennessee, targeted for completion in 2030. DCD reports up to $100 million in equity and in-kind engineering support, subject to regulatory approvals. The project would supply the TVA grid under a power agreement supporting Google data centers. An expanded engineering and construction team is a delivery milestone, not electricity available today.

DCD project report

In Practice · Track the dependency, not just the partner

A supplier announcement can improve delivery capability without closing permitting, construction, testing or grid-interface dependencies. Keep the capacity date tied to those gates, with a clear distinction between the target and demonstrated readiness.

03Incidents, Reliability & Remediation

New reporting on an older incident · London power loss became a prolonged application outage

September 21 reporting revisits Stakefish's August 8 outage. DataCentral, citing the operator's postmortem, reports more than 6,000 validators affected and full recovery after roughly 21 hours and 36 minutes. Planned electrical work removed one supply path before a breaker trip; equipment concentration and delayed failover compounded the impact. The report explicitly leaves conflicting facility labels unresolved. These are attributed findings, not independently established fault ownership, and completed remediation has not been verified.

DataCentral incident analysis

In Practice · Set the decision clock before maintenance

A recovery team needs a threshold for reassessing “wait for the facility” against its approved failover option. Agree what evidence, elapsed time and decision authority trigger that reassessment; silence from another party is not a recovery estimate.

04DC Infrastructure

Motivair adds a corridor-based CDU for hybrid cooling

September 23. Schneider Electric launched the WCDU, designed to integrate with fan-wall cooling or operate independently. It advertises up to 3.5 MW at specified flow conditions and approach temperatures as low as 2°C. Those are manufacturer ratings, not a blanket operating guarantee. Shipping is planned in selected regions in October, with US pre-orders beginning in early 2027. The practical development is packaging that supports mixed air/liquid deployments while keeping service access outside the white space.

Schneider launch announcement

In Practice · Match the commercial date to the site

“Launching now” and “available for this region's installation window” are different milestones. Confirm the applicable configuration, delivery commitment and acceptance conditions before placing the equipment on the critical path.

05DCOS, DCIM, BMS & Controls

Security catch-up · Desigo graphics files can cross a workstation trust boundary

Siemens' September 8 advisory, surfaced again in September 22 industry coverage, identifies client code execution in Desigo CC Installed and ClickOnce clients V6/V7. A malicious graphics document must be opened by a sufficiently privileged user. Siemens lists no fix for those affected clients and recommends restricting Graphics configuration access according to least privilege; its product table distinguishes unaffected client variants. This is a building-management client issue, not evidence that a particular data center was attacked.

Siemens advisory and product matrix

In Practice · Inventory the client, not only the platform

A server's product name is insufficient to establish exposure. Capture client type, version, who can import or edit graphics, and the supplier-supported mitigation. Coordinate any change with the security and operations owners; do not improvise changes on production controls.

06AI & Compute Demand

Crusoe reports Blackwell Ultra training validation in Iceland

September 21. Crusoe's blog says its NVIDIA HGX B300 deployment earned Exemplar Cloud validation using 512 GPUs across eight models and thirteen precision configurations. It describes the site as geothermal-powered and the GPUs as available through Crusoe Cloud. The announcement is evidence of a supplier-reported workload qualification, not booked customer demand or a guarantee for every training job. Independent confirmation of the specific results was not located in this pass.

Crusoe engineering blog

In Practice · Define the scale you are accepting

Component benchmarks do not establish cluster behavior. Record the tested workload, GPU count, precision and software configuration, then identify what remains unproven for the intended deployment. Keep performance qualification separate from availability and reliability commitments.

07New AI Models & Releases

Anthropic releases Opus 5.5 with a lower-cost pitch

September 22. Anthropic introduced Claude Opus 5.5 and lists standard input/output pricing of $4/$20 per million tokens. It claims typical workloads cost 40% less than Opus 5 through both pricing and efficiency improvements. The company also cautions that benchmark margins do not fully describe real-world differences. Treat the launch and listed pricing as product facts, while keeping performance and savings claims attributed to the vendor.

Anthropic release

In Practice · Specify the stopping rule

For a document task, define the deliverable, acceptable evidence and checks up front. Extra research and revision loops consume resources even with a cheaper model. Evaluate the completed artifact and total run cost, not only the per-token price.

08Networking

Meta plans a petabit transatlantic cable for 2029

September 23. Meta's Petal project would connect the US and France over approximately 7,000 km, with NEC and Sumitomo Electric involved and Orange managing the French landing. DCD describes a 24-fiber-pair system using two-core fiber, with interfaces that split and recombine the cores around amplification. The petabit capacity is a planned system milestone; it is not current lit capacity or an individual customer's throughput entitlement.

DCD cable report

In Practice · Separate capacity from diversity

More bandwidth on one route does not create a second failure domain. A resilience review still needs landing, terrestrial backhaul and shared-route information, plus the commercial capacity actually available to the service.

09Backend / Cloud & Data

UiPath agents gain generally available live structured context

September 22. UiPath now lets agents use Data Fabric entities as structured context, querying live or near-real-time records rather than relying only on static snapshots. Its release notes say existing enterprise permissions remain enforced. That can reduce stale-context problems, but neither access control nor freshness alone establishes that an agent's interpretation is correct.

UiPath release notes

In Practice · Test a denied request too

A useful acceptance case proves the agent can retrieve an allowed record and cannot expose a disallowed one. Also check how it explains missing or stale information. A successful demo with an administrator account does not demonstrate the ordinary user's experience.
