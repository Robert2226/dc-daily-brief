01Equinix

FuriosaAI uses Lisbon as a European deployment base

September 15 reporting from an Equinix Korea media briefing. DigitalToday reports that FuriosaAI installed servers using its Renegade accelerator in an Equinix Lisbon data center, with customer onboarding underway roughly a month after deployment. The stated route is to support European proofs of concept and subsequent commercial deployments. This provides a concrete example of the ecosystem around alternative accelerators; it does not establish fleet-wide performance or a published capacity commitment.

DigitalToday reporting, Japanese edition

In Practice · Port the workload, then compare it

A different accelerator changes more than the hardware invoice. Check model support, numerical behavior, compiler/runtime compatibility and operational tooling using the same representative task. Measure the cost of a successful result after integration, including engineering effort and any fallback path.

02Google

Google joins an alliance for flexible AI electricity demand

Announced September 16; DCD reported September 17. Google, NVIDIA and Emerald AI launched the AI Energy Management Alliance, joined by participants from computing and energy. DCD reports that Google is also releasing studies on translating flexible-load concepts into grid planning and operations. This is a coordinated policy and technical initiative, not a blanket approval for faster connections. Google's cited 1 GW of demand-response commitments predates this launch.

DCD report · Alliance

In Practice · Measure at the promised boundary

Pausing 5 MW of IT work does not automatically reduce the utility meter by exactly 5 MW. Cooling, electrical losses, batteries and other loads affect net withdrawal. Define the measurement point, response interval, sustained duration and recovery profile before treating flexibility as dependable capacity.

03Competitors

NorthC advances a smaller Frankfurt expansion

September 17. NorthC says preparatory construction has begun on FRA01 at Mainzer Landstraße, a planned 6 MW facility whose first phase is due in January 2028. DCD also describes the operator's broader Frankfurt portfolio. The scale is a useful counterpoint to gigawatt announcements: regional and metropolitan capacity additions remain part of the competitive market. The opening date remains a target.

DCD reporting

In Practice · Compare capacity in its market

Six megawatts near existing customers and network ecosystems can address a different need from remote bulk compute. Compare customer fit, available service dates, density and connectivity before ranking projects by advertised MW. A market pipeline should distinguish each phase from the eventual campus total.

04DC Infrastructure

Aligned breaks ground on Project Phoenix in Pennsylvania

September 17 reporting. The planned 2 GW, three-facility campus is in Shippingport, beside a former coal power station being redeveloped for natural-gas generation. Aligned says its campus will use dedicated on-site power rather than the local grid. Groundbreaking is a delivery milestone; it does not mean the full campus or its energy supply is operational. The arrangement places generation integration, fuel availability and load response squarely inside the program's dependencies.

DCD reporting

In Practice · Ask how quickly the load arrives

A plant's ability to carry 20 MW continuously says little about its response when several megawatts appear in a fraction of a second. Request a load profile and a voltage/frequency recovery envelope alongside the steady-state rating. Battery support and staged loading also have operating limits.

Study generator load acceptance

05DCOS, DCIM, BMS & Controls

ScadaLTS advisory separates new findings from an older fix

Published September 16, with its solution section updated September 17. Tenable identifies vulnerabilities in a vendor-provided ScadaLTS 2.8.1 release-candidate build 0. It now says it knows of no released version fixing these findings; the download based on 2.7.8.1 addresses a separate earlier advisory. That distinction matters when matching an installed build to remediation evidence. Do not generalize the release-candidate finding to every deployed version.

Tenable advisory and updated solution

In Practice · Inventory the function and the build

Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) is a supervisory layer; local controllers execute equipment logic. A Building Management System (BMS), electrical monitoring and a historian have different roles from Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) asset/capacity tools or a Data Center Operating System (DCOS) workflow layer. Record the actual component, version and exposed interface before connecting an advisory to an operational consequence.

06AI & Compute Demand

MLPerf Inference 6.1 widens the evidence available for procurement

September 16. MLCommons reports submissions from 30 organizations and a largest submitted system of 512 accelerators. The round includes available products alongside preview NVIDIA Rubin systems; over half of submitters used an API-oriented test harness. These results show a broader range of evaluated serving architectures, not booked customer demand or a guarantee of application performance. Compare the workload, scenario, accuracy target and system size before interpreting any headline throughput.

MLCommons results announcement

In Practice · Normalize the useful output

Two systems may produce different token rates because their models, response lengths or latency constraints differ. Start with a representative request mix and an acceptable response-time distribution. Then compare completed, correct requests within that boundary per unit of cost and capacity.

07New AI Models & Releases

Google releases Gemini 3.8 Live and Live Extended Thinking

September 15. The two live-dialogue models emphasize efficient conversation and more demanding multi-step work, respectively. Google describes visual grounding and background tool execution while dialogue continues. Its published benchmark results are evidence about those evaluations, rather than proof that a particular business workflow is reliable. The practical test is whether a voice agent can complete the task correctly while handling interruptions and tool delays.

Official launch

In Practice · Test the interrupted action

In a voice workflow, “actually, cancel that” may arrive while a tool is still running. Evaluate whether the application distinguishes an acknowledged request, an action in progress and a completed external change. Fluent narration should never be the only evidence that the requested outcome happened.

08Networking

Telxius deploys Nokia coherent pluggables across terrestrial routes

September 15 announcement. The companies describe an ICE-X 800G ZR/ZR+ deployment across Europe, the United States and Latin America, using IP-over-DWDM: carrying router traffic directly over wavelength-based optical transport. Nokia also cites a separate BRUSA subsea trial that achieved 400 Gb/s per wavelength over more than 5,600 km. The product's 800G label and the trial's 400G operating rate are different facts.

Nokia announcement

In Practice · Rate, reach and margin travel together

A transceiver's maximum line rate does not apply to every fiber path. Distance, accumulated noise and the required operating margin constrain the usable mode. Record the qualified rate for the actual route and its failure-restoration path, rather than copying the module's headline speed into the service plan.

Cirrascale announces its production inference platform

September 15. The company says its available platform combines model routing, private-model use, fine-tuning, spend controls and accelerator selection across several vendors. Those integration claims make workload portability and operational behavior useful evaluation targets. They do not by themselves demonstrate equal performance or interchangeable features across every model/hardware combination.

Cirrascale release

In Practice · Reserve capacity for useful work

When requests exceed service capacity, an unlimited queue converts a short surge into long delays. An admission policy can reject, defer or downgrade work before it consumes scarce execution resources. The correct choice depends on the user's deadline and whether a cheaper result is still acceptable.

Study inference admission, batching and deadlines

10Program & PM

ABC finds a record share of members working on data centers

September 15 release, based on an August 20–September 4 member survey. Roughly one in six members is under contract for data-center work. Reported backlog was 9.9 months for contractors with that work versus 8.3 months for those without it. These are survey aggregates, not the lead time for a particular trade or supplier. The result supports checking the relevant contractor market before accepting an unconstrained resource plan.

ABC survey release and methodology links

Scotland's planning debate adds a delivery dependency

September 16. DCD reports that Parliament backed amended wording against taking decisions on major data-center applications until planning guidance is completed, while ministers rejected the proposed blanket moratorium. The article records differing interpretations of the immediate effect. Treat the development as a reason to verify the applicable decision path, rather than assuming every application is cancelled or already subject to identical treatment.

DCD reporting and debate links

In Practice · Put the forecast beside comparable outcomes

A detailed schedule can still omit recurring approval or integration delays. Compare its critical intervals with completed projects using the same start and finish definitions. Investigate the difference before treating the most attractive date as the most credible one.
