01Equinix

Analysis: place inference around the whole workflow

September 10, an uncovered lookback article. Equinix examines model selection and execution location as related decisions: an agent can make many model and tool calls, so its total cost and latency depend on the chain rather than a single token price. This is company analysis supporting its distributed-infrastructure strategy, not a newly commissioned deployment or independently demonstrated saving. Its useful implication is to evaluate where data, tools and models exchange information before choosing the hosting location.

Equinix analysis

In Practice · Multiply the serial delay

In a hypothetical workflow with eight sequential remote calls, an extra 25 milliseconds per round trip adds 200 milliseconds before model processing or queuing. Parallel calls behave differently. Trace the actual dependency chain before assuming that moving just the model nearer the user solves response time.

Morgan State adds access to Google research compute

September 10, an uncovered lookback announcement. Morgan State University and Google Public Sector describe a collaboration that gives researchers access to high-performance GPU resources through Google's Program for Accelerated Research. The intended applications include AI, cybersecurity, climate and health research. The announcement connects infrastructure access to the university's research ambitions; it does not publish a dedicated campus power rating or demonstrate that the hoped-for institutional outcomes have been achieved.

Joint announcement

In Practice · Turn access into a usable experiment

A research team needs more than a compute entitlement: representative data, permitted access, working software and enough uninterrupted execution time must coincide. For a trial, measure time from approved experiment to reproducible result. That exposes constraints hidden by a simple count of available accelerators.

In Practice · Test the funding dependency

Compare a project's next irreversible commitment with the date its funding becomes available. An investor roadshow, a signed financing agreement and cash available for construction are different states. A credible base schedule should identify which work can continue if the financing date moves.

Thor submits a 150 MW Georgia proposal

September 14 reporting. Thor Equities has submitted plans for the Kingston Technology Hub in Bartow County, with a reported estimated cost of $1 billion and a 2030 completion target. DCD links the state development-review record. These are proposed scale, cost and timing, rather than approved, energized or customer-ready capacity. DCD reporting · Georgia project record

In Practice · Ask for the transition as well as the steady state

A cooling plant may carry the full load once running yet need time to recover after a disturbance. A capacity review should include the changing load and cooling output during that interval. Installed megawatts alone do not establish how long temperatures remain acceptable.

05DCOS, DCIM, BMS & Controls

September advisories reach building supervision and electrical monitoring

September 8 advisory cycle, uncovered in this edition. Canada's Cyber Centre lists affected Siemens families including Desigo CC, Industrial Edge Management and Reyrolle 7SR5. SecurityWeek's broader September roundup also identifies Schneider Electric's EcoStruxure IT Data Center Expert and PowerLogic T300, alongside AVEVA and Rockwell products. Product-specific versions and conditions matter; this is a reason to reconcile installed components with their advisories, not a claim that every installation is affected.

Cyber Centre advisory · Independent roundup

In Practice · Put the defect in its actual layer

A Building Management System supervises building equipment; local controllers execute configured loops and protections. Data Center Infrastructure Management adds asset and capacity context, while a Data Center Operating System may coordinate operational workflows. A historian stores observations and electrical monitoring tracks power behavior. Determine which component and communication path an advisory affects before estimating service impact or sequencing a change.

In Practice · Define useful compute by its deadline

A weather model that finishes after the forecast publication window can miss its operational purpose despite excellent throughput. 07New AI Models & Releases

Sakana releases Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2

September 11. Both are available through the company's API. They use model orchestration: a system coordinates a pool of models instead of relying on one model for every step. Max emphasizes cost efficiency, while Ultra v2 emphasizes demanding multi-step tasks. Sakana publishes benchmark comparisons, including an internal coding benchmark; these remain developer evaluations. A stable API shape does not establish unchanged behavior, data routing or dependency exposure.

Official release and evaluations

In Practice · Evaluate the complete run

Compare successful tasks, elapsed time and total spend across a fixed set of representative requests. A low token rate can still produce an expensive workflow if the system retries or delegates frequently. Include a case in which one dependency is unavailable to understand whether fallback preserves the required result.

08Networking

VIAVI expands the testing story around 1.6T networks

September 9 announcement, uncovered lookback; demonstrations are planned for September 21–23 at ECOC. VIAVI describes fabric validation, optical manufacturing tests and field certification, including its TestCenter D2 and ONE-1600 platforms. The practical development is coverage across different stages of network delivery, rather than one higher-speed link claim. VIAVI announcement

In Practice · A clean fiber is only the first layer

Connector condition and optical power help establish the physical path. Packet loss, congestion behavior and workload completion test additional layers. Passing a cable test cannot prove that a synchronized AI workload will avoid a fabric bottleneck. Match each acceptance claim to the layer actually exercised.

09Backend / Cloud & Data

Redis fixes an operator defect that could erase credentials

September 2026 maintenance notes; the page does not specify an exact release day. Redis Software for Kubernetes 8.0.20-27 fixes an Active-Active configuration-update defect. Unrelated updates could clear authentication material, making new legitimate connections fail while permitting unauthenticated connections; existing sessions could continue and health stayed normal. Redis lists fixes on several supported release lines and says upgrading repairs affected databases. Check the applicable version path in the vendor notes.

Redis release notes

In Practice · Test both sides of the boundary

After a change in an isolated rehearsal, a new connection with valid credentials should work and one without credentials should fail. An existing connection answers neither question. This is a compact way to detect a security regression that a process-liveness check can miss.

Study security rules that must survive configuration changes

10Program & PM

A passport-services dispute turns on changed commercial exposure

September 14 reporting about earlier tender events. Iron Mountain alleges that revised service-credit terms prevented it from obtaining approval to bid for a £140 million UK Home Office document-management contract. DCD reports the department's position, as recounted in the filings, that the revisions corrected inadequate terms. These are contested positions, not a court finding. The transferable delivery issue is that changed deductions and caps can alter the economics of an otherwise unchanged service scope.

DCD reporting

In Practice · Reopen the decision when its inputs change

If a performance formula changes, update its financial scenarios and operational assumptions before relying on the previous approval. A late change to exposure is substantive even when the equipment list and delivery date stay the same.
