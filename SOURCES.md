# Research watchlist — expandable, not exhaustive

Use this as a starting point on every run alongside broad web discovery. Follow links
to direct articles, announcements, release notes, and technical references; do not use
homepages as evidence for a specific claim. Add productive sources as they emerge.

## Core categories
- Equinix: https://newsroom.equinix.com/ and https://blog.equinix.com/
- Google (check all three): https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/ ; https://datacenters.google/discover-more/latest-news/ ; https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/inside-google-cloud/whats-new-google-cloud
- Crusoe (check each run): https://www.crusoe.ai/ ; https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom ; https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/blog . Follow current resource links from the homepage if a path fails.
- Independent coverage: Reuters, AP, Data Center Dynamics, Data Center Knowledge,
  Light Reading, The Register, IEEE Spectrum, and other credible specialist publications.
- Competitors: broad company/campus/financing searches; expand beyond incumbent colo
  operators to hyperscalers, regional operators, and emerging AI infrastructure providers.
- Infrastructure: Uptime Institute, ASHRAE, OCP, utility/regulator publications and equipment manufacturers.
- Models: official lab announcements, model/system cards, release notes, primary papers;
  independently reported limitations and deployment evidence when available.
- Networking: standards bodies, network and optical vendors, operator deployments and incident reports.
- Backend/cloud: AWS What's New, Azure updates, Google Cloud, CNCF projects, database
  releases and platform engineering sources. Avoid repeating Google stories elsewhere.
- Program/PM: PMI, APM, government delivery guidance, NASA systems engineering and
  credible case studies. Durable learning is not mislabeled as today's news.

## DCOS, DCIM, BMS & Controls
DCOS means Data Center Operating System; DCIM means Data Center Infrastructure
Management. Product labels overlap and are not proof of identical functionality.
Research the actual control, monitoring, inventory, capacity, integration, and workflow
boundaries of each product. This list is neither a ranking nor an approved-vendor list.

Starting ecosystems include AVEVA, Rockwell Automation/FactoryTalk, Emerson, Schneider
Electric, Siemens, Honeywell, Johnson Controls, ABB, Vertiv, Sunbird, Nlyte, Eaton,
Tridium, Modius and relevant integrators or newer providers. Do not restrict hardware coverage
to any named brand: discover controllers, I/O, instrumentation, meters, gateways,
actuators, industrial switches and other components through the story's requirements.

Useful starting pages:
- https://www.aveva.com/en/industries/infrastructure/data-centers/
- https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us.html (news and FactoryTalk release documentation)
- https://www.emerson.com/en/corporate/industries/data-centers
- https://www.sunbirddcim.com/media-press
- https://blog.se.com/dcim/
- https://www.siemens.com/ (data center, building automation and press resources)
- https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories
- https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final
- https://bacnet.org/ and https://opcfoundation.org/

On each run search combinations of `data center operating system`, `DCIM`, `BMS`,
`building automation`, `SCADA`, `EPMS`, `historian`, `controls`, `release`, `integration`,
`acquisition`, `security advisory`, and the coverage dates. Also search named vendors.
Read a mix of vendor and independent sources, and broaden the watchlist when relevant
players appear. Do not mechanically require every vendor homepage on every run.

News belongs in this section when supervisory software, controls, operational data,
or their integration is the main development. Cooling/power equipment belongs in DC
Infrastructure unless controls are the central issue; general network developments
belong in Networking. Cross-reference without copying a story into multiple sections.
