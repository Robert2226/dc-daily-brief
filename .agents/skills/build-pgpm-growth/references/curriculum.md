# PgPM Growth Curriculum

Use this curriculum to select two connected topics per edition. Advance a topic through the maturity levels in order when practical; do not force a topic merely to complete a sequence.

## Maturity levels

1. **Foundation** — Define the concept, recognize when it applies, and use a basic tool.
2. **Applied practice** — Use the concept in a realistic data-center delivery or operations scenario.
3. **Cross-functional leadership** — Coordinate competing teams, incentives, dependencies, and decisions.
4. **Strategic judgment** — Frame enterprise tradeoffs, advise sponsors, govern a portfolio, and protect value.

## Learning tracks

| Track | Foundation | Applied practice | Cross-functional leadership | Strategic judgment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stakeholders | Identify interests and influence | Build an engagement plan | Resolve competing incentives | Shape sponsor alignment and coalition support |
| Communication | Write clear updates and asks | Tailor messages by audience | Communicate uncertainty and tradeoffs | Advise executives with concise recommendations |
| Executive presence | Prepare and speak with structure | Lead a decision-focused meeting | Stay composed through challenge | Establish trust during ambiguity or escalation |
| Organization | Capture commitments and decisions | Run a personal operating system | Coordinate shared cadence and artifacts | Design lightweight governance at scale |
| Governance | Clarify decision rights | Use gates and acceptance evidence | Govern exceptions and change | Match governance depth to risk and value |
| Risk and dependencies | Distinguish risks, issues, and assumptions | Quantify exposure and define triggers | Manage cross-program dependencies | Evaluate systemic and portfolio risk |
| Vendors and customers | Clarify outcomes and responsibilities | Manage SLAs, deliverables, and acceptance | Resolve multi-vendor interfaces | Structure partnerships and commercial tradeoffs |
| Business acumen | Connect work to business outcomes | Compare cost, schedule, quality, and risk | Build a defensible business case | Prioritize investment and explain value realization |
| Data-center delivery | Understand lifecycle and critical path | Coordinate design, build, commission, and handover | Standardize delivery across sites | Govern capacity, capital, and market sequencing |
| Operational handoff | Define readiness and ownership | Prove procedures, staffing, spares, and monitoring | Close construction-to-operations gaps | Build repeatable operational-readiness systems |
| Program leadership | Separate project outputs from program outcomes | Coordinate benefits and dependencies | Lead through influence across functions | Align programs to strategy and portfolio choices |

## Pairing guidance

Prefer pairs that reinforce each other, such as:

- Stakeholders + communication
- Executive presence + decision governance
- Organization + dependencies
- Risk + professional communication
- Vendors and customers + acceptance governance
- Business acumen + capacity planning
- Data-center delivery + operational handoff
- Program leadership + value realization

Vary the pairings over time. A Daily Action should combine both topics when possible and produce an artifact Robert can reuse, such as a stakeholder map, one-page update, decision brief, RAID entry, dependency map, RACI, acceptance checklist, meeting agenda, or business-case comparison.
